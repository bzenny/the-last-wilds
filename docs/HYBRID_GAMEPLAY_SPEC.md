# Hybrid B+C Gameplay Specification v0.3

**Decision: approved direction.** Pausable real-time tactical guardian commands + living territory management.

## Core loop
Observe human intent -> command guardians -> decide whether to defend, disrupt or restore -> simulate consequences -> adapt to new human plans. World and guardians are mutually dependent: healthy habitats sustain guardians; guardians defend habitats.

## Control budget
One-click selection; right-click move/priority target; one primary ability per guardian initially; pause at any time. No high-frequency micro. Limit initial active guardians to three.

## Roles as decisions, not stats
- Rootling: snare incoming logger **or** regenerate biomass. The player chooses immediate protection vs future resilience.
- Brute: hold chokepoint **or** dismantle an outpost. Choose local safety vs strategic disruption.
- Spitter: deny road **or** harass a distant supply convoy. Choose territorial control vs slowing expansion.

Every guardian has an autonomous stance: hold, intercept, or retreat. Ability costs and cooldowns are visible. No dominant universal unit.

## Human intentions
Logger: selects accessible forest and harvests; reroutes or retreats if threatened.
Guard: escorts civilians; prioritizes credible threats, not indiscriminate attack.
Settler: seeks supplied, buildable tiles; attempts road/outpost construction and abandons unsafe sites.
Plans are telegraphed with target tile and action countdown.

## World as strategic participant
Each tile stores biome, traversability, biomass, fertility, moisture, slope, and infrastructure. Roads reduce movement cost for humans but fragment habitat. Rivers obstruct or channel routes; bridges become strategic chokepoints. Dense forest shelters guardians but may block ranged attacks. Regrowth depends on moisture and fertility. Wild Energy is generated by connected healthy habitat.

## Environmental escalation
MVP: forest, clearing, road, river and rocky high ground; weather represented by one visible modifier (rain slows road building, boosts regrowth). Later: seasons, migration, fire, erosion, corruption, and wildlife food webs. Avoid invisible penalties.

## Balancing invariants
- A single monster cannot solve every encounter.
- Every human plan has at least one readable counter.
- Regrowth must be strategically viable, not cosmetic.
- A habitat lost may be reclaimed at meaningful cost.
- Both defense and disruption can achieve success.

## First playable vertical slice
One 32x24 forest map; sanctuary; three guardians; logger/guard/settler; one road; five escalating encounters; Wild Energy; habitat health; pause; simple victory and defeat. Placeholder tiles and shapes.

## Acceptance tests
1. Players can distinguish all three guardian roles without tooltips after first demonstration.
2. Players can predict where the next human expansion occurs.
3. Players can identify a tactical and ecological response to the same threat.
4. At least two different viable paths lead to success in a seeded scenario.
5. World changes remain visible and explainable.
6. Pausing and commands never advance simulation unexpectedly.

## Not yet complete
This is a design specification. User tests, behavior tuning, and the interactive vertical slice remain to be implemented.
